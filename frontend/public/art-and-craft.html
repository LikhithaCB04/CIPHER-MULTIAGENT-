Made slowly, by hand

Rang Studio was started by a small group of artists who wanted to make things that feel personal. Every painting, pot and paper piece is made in our studio, using natural and recycled materials wherever we can.

We also teach. Our workshops are friendly and unhurried, for beginners, families and anyone who wants to make something with their hands.
