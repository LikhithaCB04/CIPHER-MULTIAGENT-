Common questions

Quick answers for new parents.

When do admissions open?

Applications for 2027–28 open on 1 November 2026 and close on 31 January 2027.

What documents do I need?

Birth certificate, previous report card, transfer certificate (for Grade 2 and above), and two passport photos.

Does the school offer transport?

Yes. Our buses cover most areas of the city. Ask the office for route details.

Can we visit the campus?

Yes. Campus tours run every Saturday at 10 am. Send an enquiry below to book.
